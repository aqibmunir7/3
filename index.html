Our story

From a market stall to Great Underbank

Sticky Fingers is run by Adrian & Klaudyna, husband and wife, bakers and owners. They started in Stockport’s own marketplace, and now run the shop on Great Underbank — the bright yellow front you cannot miss.

The baking is Polish at heart: Karpatka and cheesecake made the way they have always been made, alongside cinnamon rolls, donuts, signature brioche, cakes and bread.

They are part of the town, too — joining neighbours and local businesses for a community clean-up of Stockport town centre. If you have only seen Sticky Fingers on Instagram, this page is the missing piece: the addresses, the hours, and what to ask for when you get there.
